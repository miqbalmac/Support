Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. No account, no ads, no tracking. Here is exactly what 3D Panda uses and why.

In short: 3D Panda has no sign-up and shows no ads. We don't use analytics or tracking. We only handle what's needed to deliver models, run the AI Studio and manage your subscription credits.

This policy explains how 3D Panda ("3D Panda", "we", "us") handles information when you use the 3D Panda app for Mac (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Anonymous app ID

When the App first starts, our subscription provider (RevenueCat) creates a random ID for your copy of the App. It is not your name, email or Apple ID. We use it to know your plan and how many credits you have left.

Downloads and credits

When you download a model or use a Studio tool, our server records the anonymous app ID, which model or tool it was, and the time. This lets us count credits, let you re-download a model you already have, and prevent abuse (for example, daily limits).

AI Studio content

When you use Image to 3D or Text to 3D, the text you type or the picture you choose is sent to our server and to our AI provider to create your model. We store the prompt, the picture and the generated model so the result can be delivered to you. Only send pictures you have the right to use, and please don't include personal information in prompts.

The Logo to 3D and Text & Font to 3D generators run entirely on your Mac. The logo or text you enter is not uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat tells the App which plan is active and when it renews.

Basic technical information

Like any online service, the servers that host our content may briefly log technical details of requests, such as your IP address and the time, for security and reliability. We don't use these to identify you.

2. What stays on your Mac

  • Downloaded model files, saved in your Downloads/3D Panda folder (or a folder you choose).
  • My Library, your likes and saved collections.
  • Your settings, such as theme, slicer and download folder.
  • A cache of model pictures and previews, so the App loads faster and works offline.

Notifications are created on your Mac and are not sent through our servers. When you open a model in a slicer, the file is handed to that app on your Mac.

3. What we don't collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account. You never sign in.
  • No location, contacts or photo library. The App only reads a picture when you pick one.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and no advertising ID.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling of data.

4. How we use information

  • To deliver the models you download and keep the catalog working.
  • To create your AI models and return them to you.
  • To check your plan and count credits.
  • To prevent abuse, keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

6. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the providers above, as needed to run the App, or when the law requires it.

7. Retention

Download and credit records are kept while they are needed to manage credits, re-downloads and abuse prevention. AI Studio prompts, pictures and results are kept so you can get your models again. We may delete older records at any time. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

8. Your choices

  • Delete local data: delete the App and your Downloads/3D Panda folder.
  • Delete server data: email us and we will remove the AI Studio content and records linked to your copy of the App. Tell us roughly when you used the App and what you made, so we can find them.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information. Contact us and we will help.

9. Security

The App talks to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Model files are shared through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Children

3D Panda is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. International processing

Our providers may process information on servers in other countries, with safeguards required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the App.
