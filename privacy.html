Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026. No account and no personal details needed.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use 3D Handy Panda, our 3D printing app for Mac (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

You don't need to create an account or share any personal details to use the App. To run the App, we only handle limited technical information:

  • A random, anonymous app ID created when the App first starts (not your name, email or Apple ID)
  • Which models you download and which Studio tools you use, to count your credits
  • Your subscription status (active plan and renewal), as reported by the App Store
  • The picture or text you choose to send when you use Image to 3D or Text to 3D

2. Service Providers

The App uses a few trusted services to work: Supabase hosts the model catalog, files and credit records; RevenueCat checks your subscription; and an AI provider turns your Image to 3D and Text to 3D requests into models. They handle data only to provide these services, under their own privacy policies, and your information is not sold.

3. 3D Models and Downloads

Browsing, viewing or downloading 3D models does not require you to give us any personal information. Downloaded files are saved on your Mac, in the folder you choose, according to your Mac's storage and permission settings.

4. How We Use Information

The information above is used only to keep the App working: to deliver models, count credits, create your AI models, keep the service stable and secure, fix problems and improve future versions. It is not used for anything beyond running the App.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or ask for:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts or address book
  • Precise location
  • Your photo library (a picture is only sent if you pick it yourself in Image to 3D)
  • Camera or microphone recordings
  • Payment or card details
  • Health information

All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Our service providers can access only the limited data they need to run the App. We may disclose information only if the law requires it.

7. Data Retention

Technical information is kept only as long as needed to run the App and meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete the data linked to your copy of the App at any time.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures, such as encrypted connections, to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Third-Party Services

Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple and our AI provider follow their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

11. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information on servers in other countries, with the safeguards required by law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at miqbal.mac@gmail.com.
