About 3D Panda

3D Panda is a Mac app for people who love 3D printing. Every model in our catalog is checked and set up for printing, so you spend your time printing, not fixing files. Download a model and it opens straight in your slicer.

Want something one of a kind? AI Panda Studio turns a photo or an idea into a printable model, and makes keychains, badges and name plates from a logo or a word.
