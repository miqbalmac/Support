Frequently asked questions

Where do my downloaded models go?

They're saved in your Downloads/3D Panda folder and listed in My Library. You can choose a different folder in Settings → Downloads & files.

How do I open a model in my slicer?

Choose your slicer in Settings (Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, Creality Print or UltiMaker Cura). After a download, press Open in slicer, or turn on "Open after downloading" to open it automatically. No slicer yet? They're all free to download.

What are credits?

Downloads and Studio tools use credits. The Free plan includes a few credits each month, and every Pro plan includes more for each billing period. Downloading a model you already downloaded this period is free. You can see your credits in the sidebar.

My AI model failed. Did I lose credits?

No. If an AI generation fails, its credits are returned automatically. Try a clearer picture of a single object, or a more detailed description.

I bought Pro, but the App still shows Free.

Open the plans screen and press Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the App Store, click your name, then Subscriptions, or go straight to your subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I sell the things I print?

Unless a model's page says otherwise, models are for personal use. See our Terms of Use for details.

How do I delete my data?

Everything in My Library lives on your Mac. To remove AI Studio content and records from our servers, email us. See our Privacy Policy.
